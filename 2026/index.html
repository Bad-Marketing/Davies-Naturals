Davie's Naturals 2026 Growth Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon & Walmart Growth

Objective

Rebuild Davie's share of the Sweet Potato Dog Treats niche by strengthening its presence and competitiveness across Amazon search results.

Davie's continues to convert strongly once shoppers reach the listing, but declining click and purchase share across core Sweet Potato searches is contributing to lower traffic and fewer opportunities to acquire new customers and subscribers.

The Rest of 2026 priority is to strengthen Davie's presence in search results, win back category share, and rebuild customer and subscriber growth.

Current Snapshot
  • Sweet Potato Chews are now the clear focus of the Amazon business.
  • The 1 lb ASIN remains the primary sales and customer acquisition product.
  • PDP conversion remains strong, pointing to traffic acquisition rather than listing conversion as the primary opportunity.
  • Davie's is losing click and purchase share across its highest-intent Sweet Potato searches.
  • Organic presence on "sweet potato dog treats" is relevant but not strong enough to consistently compete for top placements.
  • Listing sessions have declined alongside the loss in search share.
  • Subscribe & Save remains a major part of the business, but the active subscriber base has gradually declined during 2026.
Growth Area 1: Rebuild Sweet Potato Category Share

Davie's is losing share within the niche most directly aligned with the product. The priority for the rest of 2026 is to increase search-result coverage and improve Davie's ability to win the click.

Expand Search-Result Coverage

Split the 2-pack from the current variation family and establish it as an independently rankable ASIN.

The 2-pack is already proven, with strong conversion and ~380 active subscriptions. Separating it creates a second opportunity for Davie's to appear organically and through advertising across the same high-value searches.

Position the two ASINs with different catalog roles:

  • 1 lb - Hero acquisition offer
  • 2-pack - Value/stock-up offer

Support both across the priority Sweet Potato searches to increase Davie's overall search-result coverage.

Strengthen Search-Result Presentation

Refine the 1 lb and 2-pack titles to better match how customers search and make Davie's key benefits clearer directly in the search results.

Prioritize
Sweet Potato Dog Treats Sweet Potato Chews for Dogs Single Ingredient Low Fat Made in USA Rawhide-Free

Replace lower-value language such as "Large & Flexy" where stronger search terms or product benefits can take its place.

Develop new main image versions for testing against the current image, with the goal of improving Davie's ability to stand out and win the click against leading Sweet Potato Dog Treat competitors.

Recover Priority Search Share

Concentrate SEO and advertising around the searches most directly aligned with Davie's.

Primary
sweet potato dog treats dog sweet potato treats sweet potato chews for dogs sweet potato treats for dogs
Supporting
dried / dehydrated sweet potato dog treats low fat dog treats low fat dog treats for pancreatitis sweet potato dog treats made in USA

Prioritize click share, purchase share and organic rank across these searches rather than broad impression growth.

Expand Paid Search Presence

Increase Davie's coverage of high-value advertising placements across the priority Sweet Potato searches.

Develop a new, highly engaging video ad designed to stand out within the competitive search results and create an additional path into the listing.

Advertising Strategy

Advertising's priority for September is to use the account's available TACOS headroom to rebuild rank and BSR ahead of Q4, rather than holding spend back and trying to build visibility during the events themselves.

Where the Account Stands

Recent bid increases pushed CPCs up without a corresponding lift in clicks, rank, or sales, spend rose, but TACOS grew for the wrong reason. Bids and Top-of-Search placements on the primary ranking campaign have already been pulled back toward the January-March levels, the account's strongest performing window, and high ACOS discovery campaigns have been trimmed to protect profitability. This has stabilized spend efficiency and sets the base for the next move.

Pre-Q4 Growth Push

The account has room to operate up to a 14% TACOS target and has not been using it. The priority for September is to spend into that ceiling deliberately, building rank and BSR while traffic is cheaper, so Davie's enters Q4 already visible rather than trying to buy visibility during Prime Big Deal Days and BFCM.

  • Rebuild TOS presence on the primary ranking campaign, scaling bids in stages rather than returning to previous levels in one move.
  • Use clicks and impression share as the leading signal that spend is converting into rank, not just TACOS.
  • Increase spend toward the 14% ceiling through September, checking weekly that rank and BSR are moving in response.
  • Hold the 2 pack out of heavy advertising until it is split and has its own performance data. Layering ad spend onto an unproven campaign risks diluting conversion rate for a margin benefit that hasn't been tested yet.
  • Expand on discovery campaigns after ranking push improves TACOS
Growth Area 2: Rebuild Sub&Save Growth

Subscribe & Save remains a major source of recurring revenue and customer value, but the active subscriber base has gradually declined through 2026.

The current 40% first-order Sub&Save coupon + 10% ongoing discount is already highly competitive. The priority is therefore not deeper discounting, but bringing more new customers into the brand and converting more of them into subscribers.

Rebuild New Subscriber Acquisition

Use recovered search traffic and new-customer growth from Growth Area 1 to create more opportunities to acquire new subscribers.

Maintain the current Sub&Save offer and ensure it remains consistently available across the active Sweet Potato catalog.

Monitor Subscriber Growth

Track Subscribe & Save performance throughout the rest of 2026, including new subscriptions, cancellations, and net active subscriptions, to understand whether subscriber growth improves as traffic and new customer acquisition recover.

Supporting Initiatives
Creator Connections

Continue Creator Connections as an additional customer acquisition channel for both the 1 lb hero offer and the 2-pack value offer.

Use creators to promote both pack sizes, positioning the 1 lb as the primary trial and acquisition offer and the 2-pack as the value option for repeat buyers and multi-dog households.

Q4 Event Participation

Prepare Davie's for the key Q4 Amazon events, including Prime Big Deal Days and Black Friday/Cyber Monday.

Confirm event participation and offers in advance, ensure sufficient inventory is available, and align PPC support around the event periods to maximize visibility and sales.

Content & Store Maintenance

No major PDP or Store rebuild is required.

  • Update the A+ comparison table to focus on active Sweet Potato pack sizes.
  • Remove discontinued products from the Store and content where needed.
  • Maintain existing product education and Sub&Save messaging.
  • Keep Store work to maintenance unless Advertising creates a stronger use for it.
